# The critic's rubric

The bar for this project is a **blind side-by-side against *The King of Fighters
XIII***. When reference frames are available (`reference/kof13/`), the critic
judges the blind sheet and picks a side. When they are not, the critic scores our
frame against the criteria below, which are the specific things KOF XIII does
exceptionally well.

The critic's job is to be **harsh**. A critic that says "looks good!" is useless.
Default to finding the frame short of the bar and say precisely why.

## What KOF XIII actually does well

Judge against these, not against generic "does it look nice".

1. **Silhouette legibility.** Every character reads instantly as a distinct
   shape, at speed, against a busy background. Hair and costume break the
   outline in memorable ways. If two fighters could be confused in silhouette,
   that is a failure.
2. **Deliberate shadow shapes.** Shadows read as *drawn decisions* — clean
   shapes with intent — not as the output of a lighting equation. Terminators
   land on anatomy in ways that describe form.
3. **Colour separation with a unified palette.** Characters are individually
   saturated and distinct from each other, yet the whole frame feels graded as
   one image. The failure mode is either mud (everything one hue) or chaos
   (nothing shares a palette).
4. **Confident linework.** Outlines vary in weight, sit heavier where forms
   occlude, and never read as a uniform traced border.
5. **Anatomical authority.** Muscle and mass are described with confidence.
   Lumps, blobs, mitten hands, and flipper feet are the tells of weak
   construction.
6. **Faces that act.** Faces are readable at gameplay size, with eyes that have
   a catch light and expressions that change with effort.
7. **Stage depth and life.** Multiple parallax planes, animated detail,
   atmosphere. The stage should feel like a place, not a backdrop.
8. **Impact.** Hitsparks, freeze frames, screen shake and dust that make contact
   feel violent. A hit that looks like two models overlapping is a failure.
9. **Animation that reads as drawn.** Poses hold, then snap. Even, floaty
   interpolation is the giveaway that this is 3D pretending.
10. **Finish.** No z-fighting, no seams, no clipping, no aliasing on the
    terminator, no smeared texture.

## How to score

For each criterion: **1–5**, where 3 = "competent 3D game", 4 = "clearly
professional", 5 = "I would believe this was KOF XIII". Report the score, the
single most damaging specific defect, and the one change that would most improve
the frame.

Then give a verdict: **would this frame win a blind side-by-side against KOF
XIII?** Yes or no. No hedging.

## Rules for blind mode

- The critic is shown the sheet only. It is never told which side is ours, and
  the key file is not in its context.
- It must commit to a side before any reveal.
- "They look equally good" is not a permitted answer — pick one and justify it.
